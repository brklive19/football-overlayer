Football Broadcast Overlay

A separate controller and transparent browser overlay for OBS, vMix or other browser-source software. Open the controller to manage match details, score, timer, cards and goal animations.

⚙ Open Control PanelEnter match details and operate the live scoreboard. ▣ Open OverlayTransparent 1920 × 1080 browser overlay.
Quick setup: Keep all three files in the same folder. Open control.html, then copy the overlay link and add it to OBS as a Browser Source. For best results set the source to 1920 × 1080.
